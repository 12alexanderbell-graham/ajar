Your agent shouldn't stop because you closed your laptop.

Long agent runs take minutes or hours. Until now you had two bad options.

Close the lid

The Mac goes to sleep and the run stops halfway. You come back to nothing done.

Leave it open

It drains the battery, runs hot, and your screen is on show to anyone walking past.

How it works

Set up once in under a minute. After that you don't think about it.

Install and allow it

Start your agent

Ajar spots Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Cursor Agent and others on its own. The menu tells you closing the lid is now safe.

Close the lid and go

It keeps working in your bag, uses a fraction of the power, and sleeps a few minutes after the agent stops.

It doesn't just stay awake. It saves the battery while it does.

18.3 W → 3.1 W

With the lid shut, Ajar pauses what nobody's using (other apps, idle simulators, silent audio), dims the screen to nothing and turns on Low Power Mode. Everything comes back the moment you open the lid.

Measured by Ajar's own power test on an M5 Max MacBook Pro with the lid closed and iOS simulators open: whole-laptop power with Ajar's savings off, then on, twice. Your numbers depend on what's running; Ajar measures them on your laptop too.

Built to be left alone

Only when it's needed

In When Busy mode it stays awake while an agent works and lets the laptop sleep a few minutes after it stops. Or pick Always for a download, a render, a long build.

No lock screen

Open the lid and you're straight back where you were. No password, no waiting for the Mac to wake up.

Safe in a bag

It always sleeps at your battery limit, and if the laptop gets too hot with the lid shut. Those two can only be loosened by you, never by an agent.

Puts everything back

Every change is undone when you open the lid. A small watchdog undoes them too if Ajar ever quits unexpectedly.

Check on it from your phone

Is it still working? How much battery is left? Let it sleep from the train. Works from anywhere, encrypted end to end, and nothing to install from an app store.

Your AI can run it too

Connect Claude Code, Codex or the Claude app in one click (Settings › AI Agents, or ajar connect). Agents keep the laptop awake for long jobs themselves. Paste this and your agent sets Ajar up with you:

Ajar is installed on this laptop. It keeps the machine awake with the lid closed while you work, and cuts power use while the lid is shut. You can control all of it through the ajar MCP tools (or the ajar command). Set it up for me:
1. Read Ajar's settings and power report to see every setting and what is using power right now.
2. Ask me, in one short message: which apps must keep running at full speed with the lid shut (music, calls, a local model, Docker...), and whether it should stay awake only while agents work or always.
3. Apply the strongest savings that don't touch what I named: preset max_battery, pausing simulators and background audio, low power, and per-app rules.
4. If my lid is closed, run Ajar's power test and tell me what the savings are worth in watts.
5. From now on, keep the laptop awake through Ajar before long work and release it when done.
Never turn off the heat guard or lower the battery limit; Ajar won't let you, and that's on purpose.

Questions

Is it safe to keep a laptop running in a bag?

Ajar watches the temperature. If the laptop gets hot with the lid shut it pauses everything it can, and if it stays hot for five minutes (or gets critical) it puts the laptop to sleep. It also always sleeps at your battery limit, 15% unless you change it.

Do I have to type my password when I open the lid?

No. Ajar keeps the screen dark instead of letting it sleep, so you're straight back in. If you'd rather have the lock screen, turn the screen fully off in Settings.

Won't it drain my battery?

Much less than leaving the lid open. With the lid shut Ajar pauses what you're not using and switches on Low Power Mode, and it lets the laptop sleep a few minutes after your agent stops. The menu shows how long the battery will last with the lid closed.

What does it change on my Mac?

Only what it needs while the lid is shut: sleep, Low Power Mode, screen brightness, and pausing or slowing apps you're not using. All of it is put back when you open the lid, and Settings › About › Remove Ajar takes everything out.

Which agents does it know about?

Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Cursor Agent, Copilot CLI, Amp, Aider, OpenCode, Ollama and the Claude app, found automatically. You can add any app or command-line tool (an Xcode build, ffmpeg, Blender) in Settings › Apps.

Does it cost anything?

No. Ajar is free and open source under the MIT license. The code is on GitHub.

Windows and Linux?

Yes, with the same When Busy / Always modes, battery limit, heat guard and phone control. Windows 10 and 11, and Linux desktops with systemd (GNOME, KDE and others).
