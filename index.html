What it does

Knows when Claude Code needs you

Working, waiting for your approval, or finished, straight from Claude Code.

Sleeps when the work is done

Awake only while an agent or an app you picked is busy. Or always, or for a set time.

Saves battery with the lid shut

Pauses apps you aren't using, freezes simulators and turns on Low Power Mode.

Approve everything, until it's done

One switch lets your agents run without asking. It turns itself off when the work finishes.

Battery and heat limits

Sleeps at your battery limit, or if it gets too hot in a bag.

Puts everything back

Open the lid and it's all as it was. A watchdog covers crashes.

Control it from your phone

Everything the menu bar does, from anywhere: how long it stays awake, Sleep, which apps keep it awake or get paused, and every setting. Turn on check-ins and a sleeping laptop picks up a wake from your phone at the next one, every 15, 30 or 60 minutes.

  • iPhone app: coming to the App Store.
  • Any phone, today: the phone page. Add it to your Home Screen and it opens like an app.
  • On your computer, open Nocturnal and choose Pair a Phone. End-to-end encrypted, with a key for each phone you can revoke.

Questions

Is it safe in a bag?

It sleeps if it gets hot or the battery reaches your limit. The heat guard and battery limit can't be loosened by an agent or the phone.

What does it change?

Sleep, Low Power Mode, screen brightness and idle apps, only while the lid is shut. Opening the lid puts it all back.

Can my AI agent control it?

Yes. Connect it in Settings › Agents, then give your agent this prompt.

Nocturnal is installed on this laptop. It keeps the machine awake with the lid closed while you work, and cuts power use while the lid is shut. You can control all of it through the nocturnal MCP tools (or the nocturnal command). Set it up for me:
1. Read Nocturnal's settings and power report to see every setting and what is using power right now.
2. Ask me, in one short message: which apps must keep running at full speed with the lid shut (music, calls, a local model, Docker...), and whether it should stay awake only while agents work or always.
3. Apply the strongest savings that don't touch what I named: preset max_battery, pausing simulators and background audio, low power, and per-app rules.
4. If my lid is closed, run Nocturnal's power test and tell me what the savings are worth in watts.
5. From now on, keep the laptop awake through Nocturnal before long work and release it when done.
Never turn off the heat guard or lower the battery limit; Nocturnal won't let you, and that's on purpose.
Windows and Linux?

Both work, in beta: the same engine as the Mac, with a tray icon instead of the menu bar panel. Measuring savings is Mac-only for now.

Why does it ask for a password?

Once, on the Mac, to allow only the few commands that switch sleep and Low Power Mode. Delete the app and that permission removes itself.

Lost internet with the lid shut?

On an iPhone hotspot, set System Settings › Wi‑Fi › Ask to join hotspots to Automatic.
